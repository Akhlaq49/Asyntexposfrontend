1

Sign in and find your way

For everyone · needs your email and password
  1. Open the software link in your browser (Chrome works best).
  2. Type your email address and password, then click Sign In.
  3. You land on the Dashboard: today's sales, purchases and dues at a glance.
  4. Use the left menu to move between modules. Click a heading such as Sales to see its pages.
  5. Every list page has the same tools at the top: PDF Excel Print Refresh and a search box.
  6. Each row in a list has named buttons such as View Edit Delete Print.
  7. Add New at the top of the screen is a shortcut to add a product, quotation, customer, supplier, expense or user.

To sign out, click the red Logout button at the top right, then Yes.

2

Users and roles

Admin only · needs step 1

Give each staff member their own sign-in, and decide which menus each role can see.

2.1 Set what each role can see User Management → Roles & Permissions

  1. Pick a role in Select Role, for example Sales.
  2. Tick the menus that role may use. Select All ticks everything.
  3. Click Save Permissions. The Admin role always has full access.

2.2 Add users User Management → Users · needs 2.1

  1. Click Add User.
  2. Fill in User Name, Email, Phone, Password and Confirm Password (at least 6 characters).
  3. Choose the Role, set Status to Active, and save.
  4. To change a user later, click Edit on their row. Leave the password blank to keep it.

2.3 Hide unused menus User Management → Menu Configuration · optional

Turn off menus your company does not use, so nobody sees them.

3

Company letterhead

Documents → Letterhead · needs step 1

Print a letter on company paper whenever you need one. Nothing else depends on this step.

  1. Go to Documents → Letterhead.
  2. For blank paper, click No, then Print blank letterhead.
  3. For a letter, click Yes. Fill in Ref (for example OTA/2026/015) and Date, type the letter and pick a Text size.
  4. Click Print letter. The logo, address bar and stamps are added for you. Clear starts over.
4

Product setup

Inventory menu · needs step 1

Do these in order. A product needs its unit, category and brand to exist first.

4.1 The lists products use Inventory menu

  1. Units: how you count items (Piece, Meter, Box).
  2. Category: groups such as Switches, Locks, Cameras.
  3. Brands: the makers you sell, for example Sonoff or Tuya.
  4. Tax Types: the taxes you charge, with their percentage.
  5. Warranties: warranty periods, for example 1 Year.

4.2 Products Inventory → Create Product · needs 4.1

  1. Enter Product Name, SKU, Model and Color.
  2. Choose Category, Brand, Unit and Warranty.
  3. Enter the selling Price and the opening Quantity in stock.
  4. Add a Description and images if you like, then save.
  5. See all products in Inventory → Products. Use Edit to change one.

4.3 Purchase prices Inventory → Purchase Prices · needs 4.2

  1. Find a product with the search box.
  2. Type what you pay the supplier in Purchase price. The Margin against the end-user price shows straight away.
  3. Click Save. Undo changes clears unsaved edits.

Purchase orders start from these prices, and quotations show their profit against them.

4.4 Solution packages Inventory → Solution Packages · needs 4.2

This tells the system which products make up a Standard and a Premium smart-home solution.

  1. Product Categorization: put each product in a group and tick Standard solution and/or Premium solution.
  2. Package Quantities: how many of each item a building of each size needs.
  3. Drawing Rules: which product goes with which point on a drawing (a light point needs a switch).
  4. Import: load all of this from an Excel file instead of typing it.
5

Customers and suppliers

Peoples menu · needs step 1

Add a customer before you quote them, and a supplier before you order from them.

5.1 Customers Peoples → Customers

  1. Click Add Customer.
  2. Enter the name, phone, WhatsApp, email, CNIC, city and address.
  3. Fill in the building: Building type (House, Villa, Apartment), Space (10 Marla, 1 Kanal) and Stage of the building.
  4. Save. Use Call or WhatsApp on a customer's row to contact them.

5.2 Suppliers Peoples → Suppliers

  1. Click Add Supplier.
  2. Enter the name, phone, email, city and address, then save.
6

Buying stock

Purchases menu · needs steps 4 and 5
Purchase order→Receive goods→Pay supplier→Return (if needed)

6.1 Make a purchase order Purchases → Purchase Order

  1. Click New Purchase Order, or click Purchase Order on a quotation (step 7) to order exactly what it needs.
  2. Choose the supplier, the Order date and the Expected delivery date.
  3. Add the products and quantities. Prices fill in from Purchase Prices. Add Shipping if any.
  4. Add Notes for supplier if needed.
  5. Click Save as Draft to finish later, or Save & Mark Ordered when it is sent. A draft is sent later with Mark Ordered.
  6. Click Print to give the order to the supplier.

6.2 Receive the goods same page · needs 6.1

  1. When goods arrive, click Receive on the order.
  2. Enter the Qty received for each product and the Date received. Part deliveries are fine; receive the rest later.
  3. If you pay now, enter Amount paid now and the Method. Paid in full fills the whole amount.
  4. Click Receive into stock. Stock goes up and the receipt appears in Purchases.

6.3 Pay the supplier later Purchases → Purchases · needs 6.2

  1. Find the purchase and click Pay.
  2. Enter the amount and method, then save. Payments shows what has been paid.

6.4 Return goods to a supplier Purchases → Purchase Return · needs 6.2

  1. Click Return a purchase.
  2. Type the Purchase reference and click Find.
  3. Enter Returning now for each product and a Reason / notes.
  4. If the supplier refunds you, enter Refunded by the supplier now and the Refund method.
  5. Click Save return. Stock goes down. Print gives a return slip.
7

Selling

Sales menu · needs steps 4 and 5
Drawing→Quotation→Approval→Order goods→Sale & invoice→Return (if needed)

7.1 Get a solution from the drawing Sales → Drawing to Solution · optional

  1. Upload the drawing: choose the customer's electrical drawing (PDF) and click Read Drawing. Wait until reading finishes.
  2. Check what was read: look over the floors, rooms and points, and correct anything wrong.
  3. Choose the solution: pick Standard or Premium, and say whether the client already uses another company's automation products.
  4. Read the Product breakdown per area: every product with its quantity per floor (each floor in its own colour) and room. Enter the installation charges.
  5. Click Breakdown Excel, Excel or PDF to send it, or Create Quotation to turn it into a quotation.

7.2 Make the quotation Sales → Quotation

  1. Click Add Quotation, or arrive here from 7.1 with the products already filled in.
  2. Choose the customer, add the products and quantities, and check the prices.
  3. Click Submit. No, save quotation keeps your standard terms; Yes, edit terms changes them for this quotation only.
  4. To change a saved quotation, click Revision. Each revision is a new version (R1, R2 …); Show all versions lists them.

7.3 Approval needs 7.2

  1. Staff click Send for Approval. The quotation shows Approval Pending until an admin answers.
  2. The admin opens Sales → Staff Approvals, checks it and clicks Approve or Discard.
  3. Once approved, staff can Print it. Admins can print and convert their own quotations straight away.

7.4 Order the goods needs 7.3

Click Purchase Order on the quotation. It opens a purchase order with the quotation's products. Then follow step 6.

7.5 Confirm the sale needs 7.3

  1. When the customer agrees, click Convert to Sale on the quotation.
  2. Enter any Advance received now and the Payment method, then confirm.
  3. The order now waits in Sales → POS Orders as In sale queue.
  4. When the goods have arrived (6.2), click Convert to sale on that order. Stock goes down.
  5. Click Print invoice to give the customer the bill.

7.6 Sales return Sales → Sales Return · needs 7.5

  1. Click Return a sale.
  2. Type the Sale reference (for example SL001) and click Find.
  3. Enter Returning now for each product, then save. Stock goes back up.
  4. Click Print return for the customer's copy.
8

Money

Finance menu · needs steps 6 and 7

Sales and purchases post their payments here by themselves. You only add what happens outside them.

8.1 Expenses Finance → Expenses

  1. First, in Expense Category, click Add Category and add groups such as Rent, Fuel, Salaries.
  2. Then in Expenses, add each expense with its category, amount, date and payment method.

8.2 Capital & Loans Finance → Balance Sheet → Capital & Loans

Record money the owner puts in or takes out, loans taken or repaid, and business equipment bought. Click View Balance Sheet to see the result.

8.3 Check the money needs 8.1 and 8.2

  1. Transactions: every payment in and out. Filter by Money in or Money out.
  2. Balance Sheet: what the business owns, owes and what belongs to the owner.

8.4 Accounting books Finance → Accounting · for your accountant

The books fill in automatically from every sale, purchase, return, expense and payment.

  1. Chart of Accounts: the accounts and their balances. Add Account adds one under a group.
  2. Journal: every accounting entry. Click an account to see its ledger.
  3. Trial Balance, Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet (books): the standard reports for any period.
  4. Accounting Settings: set a lock date so closed months can't be changed.

8.5 Journal vouchers Finance → Accounting → Journal Vouchers · needs 8.4

Post an entry yourself when no other screen does it: moving an amount posted to the wrong account (reclass), depreciation, amortization, accruals, or moving a balance from one customer or supplier to another. No money moves.

  1. Click New Voucher. Enter the Date and a Description saying why, for example "Reclass rent wrongly posted to Office expenses".
  2. On each line, choose the Account and type the amount in Debit or Credit. Add line adds more lines.
  3. For Receivable, Payable or Loans, also choose the customer, supplier or lender.
  4. Check the totals show Balanced, then click Save & Post. The voucher gets a number (JV-0001) and goes straight into the books.
  5. To undo a voucher but keep a record, click Reverse and pick the date. To print it, click Print.

Stock can't be used in a voucher: its value comes from purchases and sales. Dates on or before the lock date can't be used either.

Quick help

Common problems and the step that fixes them
ProblemWhat to do
A menu is missingYour role doesn't allow it. Ask the admin to tick it in Roles & Permissions (2.1).
A product isn't in the list when quoting or orderingCreate it first in Inventory (4.2).
A customer or supplier can't be chosenAdd them first in Peoples (step 5).
A price shows 0 on a quotation or drawing breakdownSet the product's price (4.2) or purchase price (4.3).
Convert to Sale is greyed outThe quotation needs an admin's approval first (7.3).
A queued order can't be converted to a saleThe goods haven't been received yet. Receive the purchase order first (6.2).
Can't print a quotationStaff can print only after approval (7.3).
Something was saved by mistakeUse Revision on a quotation, or a return for a sale or purchase. Ask the admin before deleting anything.
An amount went to the wrong accountMove it with a journal voucher (8.5): debit the right account, credit the wrong one.
Save & Post is greyed out on a voucherTotal debits and credits aren't equal yet. The difference shows under the lines (8.5).
